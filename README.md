### 🧑‍🔬 About Me


I'm a **Physics PhD** who models physical systems numerically and builds the software that makes that modeling reliable. The problems I enjoy most are statistical and computational: **phase transitions in spin systems, heat transport at the atomic scale, light moving through optical systems**, and the noisy detector data that experiments produce.


Engineering is a full career of its own for me: **15+ years** building software, data and machine-learning systems, today as a computational scientist and senior AI engineer in healthcare. I build production ML models, LLM evaluation and red-team harnesses, RAG and agentic workflows, and typed Spark/Databricks data platforms.


Whether it's a GPU Monte Carlo sweep or an LLM release, I work the same way: **model it → simulate it → validate it → make it reproducible.**

### 🚀 Featured Projects


**Physics & simulation**


- **Lattice Lab**: GPU Monte Carlo simulation of Ising and spin-lattice models, with Metropolis and cluster updates and finite-size scaling
- **Beamline Logger**: detector data acquisition and analysis pipeline, from raw readout to calibrated spectra
- **Thermoflux**: molecular-dynamics simulation of heat transport, using Green-Kubo and non-equilibrium methods
- **Optics Bench**: ray-tracing and wave-optics simulator for checking which regime an optical problem actually needs


**Applied AI**


- **Gauntlet**: adversarial LLM evaluation and red-team harness
- **ClaimSight**: predictive risk engine for healthcare claims
